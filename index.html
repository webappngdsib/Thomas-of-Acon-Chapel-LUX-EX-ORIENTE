Chapel Lux ex Oriente — Banner

Σχέδιο λάβαρου του Παρεκκλησίου και τεχνικές προδιαγραφές κατασκευής. Τα σημεία σε κόκκινο χρειάζονται επιβεβαίωση πριν την παραγωγή.
Banner design and manufacturing specification. Items in red must be confirmed before production.

ShapeVertical pennant with straight top and a pointed (V-shaped) lower edge, hung from a rod by three loops.
DimensionsTo be confirmed. Proportions of the design are 2 : 3 (width : height).
BorderDeep-red velvet border on all sides, edged inside with gold braid. Alternating embroidered gold scallop shells and gold crosses (outlined crosses) evenly spaced around the border.
Centre panelWhite damask (tone-on-tone floral pattern), framed by the gold braid.
Upper inscription“COMMEMORATIVE ORDER OF / SAINT THOMAS OF ACON” — two lines, red serif capitals with gold edge.
EmblemRed cross with gold edge and a raised gold scallop shell at its centre.
Lux ex Oriente deviceRising gold sun (half-disc with straight rays) on a gold horizon line; inside the half-disc, the Greek flag in blue and white.
Lower inscription“CHAPEL / LUX EX ORIENTE / ATHENS GREECE / EST. 2027” — red serif capitals with gold edge, the last line smaller. Place name and year to be confirmed (see below).
FringeDense gold fringe along both lower (pointed) edges.
HangingThree red velvet hanging loops; gold rod with fleur-de-lis finials.
ReverseMaterial and colour to be confirmed.

Materials and decoration

Velvet border. High-density machine embroidery in metallic gold thread with multicolour details (red, white, blue) to match the design. Reproduce all lettering, the cross, scallop shells, sun rays and flag exactly.

Fringe and finishing

Thick, dense, soft gold silk fringe with a natural drape; no plastic or rigid fringe. Reinforced seams, strong hanging loops and a neat professional finish.

Reverse backing

The entire reverse finished in satin, with no exposed unfinished seams. Colour to be confirmed.

Before production: submit a scaled proof, verify the spelling and placement of all inscriptions, and provide fabric and thread-colour samples for approval. Itemise in the quotation the rod and finials, hanging hardware, packaging, shipping to Greece and delivery time.

Materials and finishing follow the same standard as the other banners of the Orders beyond the Craft in Greece.

ΕΚΚΡΕΜΟΤΗΤΕΣ · OUTSTANDING ITEMS — CONFIRMATION REQUIRED

  • Place name: the design reads “ATHENS GREECE”, while the draft By-laws give the seat as Piraeus (24 Konstantinoupoleos Street). Confirm Athens or Piraeus.
  • Year: the design reads “EST. 2027”. Confirm against the consecration date.
  • Dimensions: confirm finished width and height, and whether they include fringe, loops and rod.
  • Reverse: confirm reverse material and colour.
